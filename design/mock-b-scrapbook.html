‹ schoolweek 7 · 2/4

"leave it" & impulse control

Biewers are quick and curious — "leave it" can save your pup from dropped meds or chicken bones on walks.

📖 lesson 3 · "leave it" — level 2 (~10 min/day)

Treat on the floor under your hand. Say "leave it" once. The instant Biru looks away — even a glance — mark "yes!" and reward from your other hand. Never give the "left" treat: ignoring that thing has to pay better.

⚠️ biewer tip: sessions under 5 min, rice-grain treats. Tiny tummy!
