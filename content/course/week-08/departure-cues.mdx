Dogs are pattern-reading machines. Keys jingle → coat goes on → human vanishes: by now Biru has the sequence memorized, and for a velcro breed each step can crank the anxiety dial before you've even left.

So scramble the data. Pick up your keys and... sit back down. Put on shoes and... make tea. Touch the doorknob and... wander off. Ten meaningless repetitions a day and the cues stop predicting abandonment; they stop predicting anything at all.

Watch him during reps: perked ears and pacing mean the cue still has power — repeat it until boredom wins. This is dull homework that pays off enormously.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers track their humans with their eyes constantly. If he's already shadowing you room-to-room all day, add "closed bathroom door" practice too — the mildest possible separation, many times a day.
