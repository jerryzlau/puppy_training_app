Time for real departures — short, boring, and verified. Set up his pen with a frozen stuffed food toy (the good stuff, alone-time exclusive). Leave without ceremony. Stand outside the door for five minutes. Come back in like it's nothing.

The critical tool here is a camera — an old phone on a shelf works. You need to *see* what alone-Biru does, because "he seemed fine" and "he cried for 14 straight minutes" look identical from outside a door. Calm chewing, wandering, napping: extend the time. Continuous crying, pacing, door-scratching: shrink the time and rebuild slower.

Grow the intervals across the week: 5, 10, 15, 20, 30. Always return before panic, always low-key on both ends.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a stuffed toy sized for toy breeds takes a biewer 20+ minutes to finish — it's not a snack, it's a babysitter. Freeze three at a time so one's always ready.
