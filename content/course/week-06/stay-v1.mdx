Stay is really three separate skills — duration, distance, distraction — and the classic mistake is training all three at once. This week: duration only, tiny doses.

Cue sit, hold a flat "wait" palm, count one-mississippi, "yes!", treat, release word ("free!"). That's a rep. Build seconds slowly: 2, 4, 6, 8. If he breaks, no drama — you asked for too much; make it easier.

Two rules that matter: pay *during* the stay (bring the treat to him), and always end with your release word so *he* never decides when stays end. Distance comes next week-ish: one step back, return, pay. One step is a big deal when your dog is four pounds and you're a skyscraper.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** keep stay sessions to 2–3 minutes max. A biewer's focus tank is small and runs on premium fuel — quit while he's brilliant.
