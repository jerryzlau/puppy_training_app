From a sit, hold a treat to Biru's nose and slowly lower it straight down to the floor between his paws, then drag it slightly forward — nose follows, elbows fold, down. "Yes!", release the treat while he's still lying down.

Small dogs often find "down" harder than big dogs — it's a vulnerable position when everything in the world towers over you. Train it on a comfy mat, never on cold slick floors, and keep your body language soft.

Fade the lure the same way as sit: real motion with empty hand, pay from the other hand, then word first, signal second. Downs are the gateway to "stay," "settle at the café," and "go chill on your mat" — worth polishing.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** if he pops back up instantly, you're paying too late — deliver the treat between his front paws *while* his belly is on the mat, so down is where the money lands.
