The crowd-slayer. From a down, lure his nose toward his shoulder blade (the week-12 roll-over path) but stop halfway — when he tips flat onto his side, mark and feed him *while flat*. Flat is the trick; payment location is everything.

Once he flops readily, attach your cue of choice: the classic finger-gun "bang!", or "go to sleep" if the household prefers its drama pacifist. Build a short hold before the release word — a dramatic death that lasts three seconds reads infinitely better than an instant pop-up.

Polish points: a slow head-drop at the end (capture it when it happens, jackpot), and releasing to huge applause. Biewers metabolize applause.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** train on soft surfaces and never push him over — flopping must be his idea. A biewer who's been tipped by hand starts dodging the trick entirely.
