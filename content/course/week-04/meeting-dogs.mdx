Dog friends matter, but for a biewer puppy the *quality* of early dog meetings beats quantity by a mile. One bad scare from a bouncy labrador can install a lifetime of leash reactivity.

Choose his classmates: calm adult dogs you know are vaccinated and gentle, ideally small or famously tolerant. Keep first greetings to three seconds — sniff, sniff, "let's go!", treat, done. Short and sweet, ending while everyone still wants more.

Learn his overwhelm signals: tail tucked, hiding behind your legs, lip licking, trying to leave. When you see them, calmly scoop him up and create distance. You are his bodyguard; being rescued *builds* confidence, it doesn't ruin it.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** skip the open dog park for at least a year (many biewer owners skip it forever). A 4-pound dog in a big-dog scrum is genuinely unsafe — curated playdates only.
