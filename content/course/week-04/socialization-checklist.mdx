Right now — roughly weeks 8 to 14 of his life — Biru's brain is running a once-in-a-lifetime program: *things I meet calmly now are normal forever; things I never meet become scary later.* This window closes. Use it.

The recipe is exposure + distance + cheese. Let him see, hear, or sniff a new thing from far enough away that he's curious rather than worried, feed a stream of tiny treats, leave while it's still going well. He does not need to interact with everything — calmly observing the garbage truck *is* the win.

Aim for a handful of new experiences a day, always at his pace. If he startles, add distance, add treats, and shrink the exposure. Never flood him.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** the world is enormous when you're four pounds. Let Biru observe scary-big things from your arms first — height is confidence for a toy breed — then work down to the ground.
