If the only place the car ever goes is the vet, Biru will do the math. Beat him to it: make the car a treat dispenser that occasionally drives somewhere wonderful.

Start parked. Carrier in, treats in, engine off, hang out, done. Next: engine on, no driving. Then a five-minute loop that ends at a park or a friend's house. You're stacking the deck so "car" predicts adventure.

He rides in a crash-tested carrier or dog car seat, secured with the seatbelt, every single time — never loose, never on a lap. Loose four-pound dogs and airbags are a horrifying combination.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** small dogs get motion-sick more easily. Keep pre-drive meals light, crack a window for airflow, and if he drools or gulps, shorten the drives and build back up.
