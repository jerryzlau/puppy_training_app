Sometime soon a professional will bathe, dry, and trim Biru — and whether that's a spa day or a trauma depends mostly on this week's prep.

Home rehearsal first: the full fake groom. Brush, handle every paw, wipe the face, and — the weird one — practice standing on a raised non-slip surface (counter with a bath mat) since grooming happens on tables. Pay throughout.

Then the genius move: the hello-only visit. Walk into the groomer's, staff hand out treats, Biru sniffs the scary-smelling place, everyone leaves. No groom. The shop stops predicting doom. First real appointment: keep it minimal (bath, tidy, nails) and brief the groomer that he's a first-timer — good groomers slow down for that.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** the blow dryer is the #1 grooming fear for small dogs. Desensitize at home: your hairdryer on cool + low, pointed *near* not at him, treats raining — a dog who's met wind before makes the groomer's day.
