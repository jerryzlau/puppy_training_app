Nail trims sit at the intersection of week 2's handling work and pure patience. The secret is decoupling every step: clippers visible ≠ clipping. Clippers touching nail ≠ clipping. One clip ≠ ten clips.

Sequence, one step per session if needed: clippers appear → treat. Clippers touch a nail → treat. Clip the very tip of ONE nail → jackpot, session over. One nail a day is a completely legitimate schedule forever — that's a full rotation every two weeks, which is exactly right.

Take only the tips (biewer nails have a quick that's easy to hit) and keep styptic powder nearby for confidence — yours, mostly. If either of you gets tense, stop; tomorrow is another nail.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers often tolerate a nail *grinder* better than clippers — no sudden pressure-crack sensation. Introduce it with the same one-step-at-a-time recipe: sound on = treats, from across the room first.
