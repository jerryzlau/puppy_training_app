Twelve weeks. From a four-pound stranger who cried at 2am to a dog who sits at doorways, comes when called, and naps under café tables. Take a second and feel that.

Today has three official duties, none skippable. **The photo:** graduation attire (a bow tie counts; a tiny mortarboard earns extra credit), best light in the apartment, forty attempts to get one where he's looking at the camera. **The celebration:** his favorite everything — the good walk, the sanctioned puppuccino, a brand-new toy he gets to destroy. **The diary entry:** title it "graduation day," attach the photo, and write a few lines to the two of you who'll read it when he's grey around the muzzle.

What's next? Everything. Adolescence (hold the line), advanced tricks, maybe therapy-dog work — biewers excel at it. But that's next quarter's syllabus. Today, you celebrate.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers live 12–16 years. The habits you built these twelve weeks — the brushing, the teeth, the training games, the diary — are the ones you'll still be doing in 2040. You didn't just train a puppy; you built a life together. Class dismissed. 🎓
