Two showstoppers. **Roll over:** from a down, lure the treat from his nose toward his shoulder blade — head follows, body tips onto one hip, keep the lure moving in an arc and momentum does the rest. Pay generously at the halfway point (on his side) for the first days; rolling belly-up is a trust exercise, and rushing it backfires.

**Sit pretty** (beg): from a sit, raise a treat just above his nose — front paws leave the ground, mark instantly at first, even for a half-second lift. Build the hold time slowly; it's a genuine core workout and strength takes weeks to develop. Let him balance against your arm at first if he wants.

These two are patience tricks — a rep or two a day beats drilling. You have the rest of his life to perfect them.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** sit pretty is actually *great* physio for small dogs (core and back strength) — but only in tiny doses while he's a puppy. Three reps a session max until he's past a year old.
