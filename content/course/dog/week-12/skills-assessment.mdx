Exam day — but the friendly kind. Run through the whole curriculum cold, one cue each, real-world conditions, and grade honestly: **pass / wobbly / needs work.**

The dirty dozen: name response · sit · down · stay (10 seconds) · come (indoors) · come (outdoors, long line) · leave it (floor treat) · drop it · loose leash (one block) · crate on cue · settle on mat (5 minutes) · alone 30 minutes.

Wobbly is normal — a 6-month-old puppy is a teenager with a learner's permit, not a finished dog. Tune up the wobblies with one short session each. "Needs work" items just become next month's practice list; training doesn't end at graduation, it just stops having a syllabus.

Then write his report card in the diary. You'll reread it in a year and cry a little. This is by design.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** adolescence (6–12 months) will temporarily *un-train* several of these — recall especially. This is neurology, not defiance. Keep paying well and hold the line; the trained adult dog re-emerges around his first birthday.
