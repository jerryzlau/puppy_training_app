Recall is the cue that can save Biru's life, and it's built on one iron law: *coming when called is ALWAYS the best deal available.* No exceptions, no fine print, forever.

Indoors, six feet away: "Biru, come!" in your happiest voice, open arms, maybe a little run-away-from-him (chasing you is irresistible). When he arrives: jackpot — three treats, one at a time, plus a party. Grab his harness gently *then* treat sometimes, so hand-reaching-for-harness never becomes the end of fun.

Play recall ping-pong with your wife: call him back and forth across the apartment, both of you paying like slot machines. Never call him for anything he hates (nail trims, crate-and-leave) — go get him for those instead.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers read enthusiasm like a lie detector. A flat, bored "come" gets a flat, bored response — commit to the silly voice. Your dignity was already gone the day you started narrating his poops.
