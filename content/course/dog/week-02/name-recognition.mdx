"Biru" should be the best word in the world. Say his name once in a happy voice; the instant he looks at you, mark it ("yes!") and treat. That's the whole game — name means look at me, and looking at me pays.

Ten reps per session, a few sessions a day, always with something great at the end. Never use his name for scoldings, baths, or nail trims in these early weeks — you're building an unbreakable "name = good" association that recall will ride on later.

If he doesn't look, don't repeat "Biru Biru BIRU" — you'll teach him the name means nothing. Get closer, get more exciting, try again.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers are sensitive to tone. A sharp or annoyed voice can make this breed shut down entirely — keep the name-game voice light and silly, always.
