For a few meals this week, skip the bowl. Feed Biru his kibble piece by piece from your hand — sometimes for nothing, sometimes for a glance at you, sometimes sprinkled while you handle a paw.

This does three quiet, powerful things: it makes human hands the source of all good things (never something to guard against), it teaches a soft mouth (if teeth touch skin, say "ouch," pause three seconds, continue), and it builds focus on your face that every future cue depends on.

Split the job between both of you. Biru should conclude that *both* his humans are treat vending machines with great taste.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a biewer's daily food ration is tiny — hand-feeding real meals means you get 60+ training reps a day without adding a single extra calorie.
