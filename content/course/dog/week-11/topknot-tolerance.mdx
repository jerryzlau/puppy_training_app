The biewer signature look — hair up, face visible, maximum drama — isn't just aesthetics. Hair in the eyes causes irritation and tear staining, so face care and the topknot are health routines wearing a cute disguise.

Daily face wiping first: soft damp cloth at the inner eye corners, one gentle swipe, treat. This prevents the rust-colored tear stains that plague white-faced breeds and keeps his eyes comfortable.

The topknot: gather → hold three seconds → release → treat, many reps, before any band appears. First real topknot: loose, small band (never rubber — snag city), five minutes, then removed *by you* before he paws it out. Gradually extend. If he learns pawing removes it, you'll fight that forever, so always beat him to the removal.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** band tension matters — too tight pulls the skin and *creates* the face-scratching you're trying to avoid. You should be able to slide a fingertip under the band's base easily.
