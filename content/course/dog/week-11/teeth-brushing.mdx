Unglamorous but critical: toy breeds have the worst teeth in the dog world. Crowded jaws, plaque that turns to disease fast, and dental extractions as a common middle-age event — unless someone brushes. That someone is you, and daily beats weekly by a mile.

Make it a dessert course, not a procedure. Dog toothpaste (poultry flavor — never human toothpaste) licked off your finger for a few days. Then your finger, paste-loaded, rubbed on the front teeth for five seconds. Then a finger-brush, then a tiny toothbrush, adding seconds and teeth gradually. Front teeth and canines are easy; back molars are the goal but come last.

Anchor it to a daily event — after the evening potty trip, before the bedtime cue — and it becomes automatic for you both.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** at his adult weight, dental disease can cost a biewer actual years of life — vets consistently name it the #1 preventable toy-breed health issue. This boring little lesson is quietly the most important one in month three.
