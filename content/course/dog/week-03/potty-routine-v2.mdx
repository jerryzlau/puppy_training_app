By now the schedule is running. This week is about the misses — because there will be misses, and how you handle them decides how fast this finishes.

If you catch him mid-squat: a cheerful "outside!", scoop him up, finish outdoors, treat like it was his idea. If you find a puddle after the fact: say nothing, clean it with enzyme cleaner, and quietly note that he was unsupervised too long. Punishment after the fact teaches exactly one thing — hide from the humans when you need to pee. That's the opposite of what you want.

Start learning his "tell": the sudden sniffing spiral, the drift toward a corner. Catching the tell is graduation-level potty training.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** small dogs have small accidents that are easy to miss under furniture — do a nose-level sweep of his zone daily, because every undiscovered accident invites a repeat visit.
