Here is the week-3 secret nobody tells you: separation anxiety is easier to prevent than cure, and prevention starts when being alone is measured in *seconds*.

Pop Biru in his pen with something great — a stuffed food toy is perfect — and simply walk out of the room. Ten seconds. Come back before he fusses. No announcement when you leave, no party when you return; comings and goings should be the most boring events in his day.

Stretch it gradually: 30 seconds, a minute, two, while you do normal things nearby. He's learning the deepest lesson of his little life: *humans leave, humans always come back, and meanwhile I have snacks.*

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers are true velcro dogs — bred for companionship and prone to shadowing you to the bathroom. Skipping this lesson is the #1 regret of toy-breed owners. Do the reps.
