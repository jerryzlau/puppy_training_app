The first nights are the hardest — for both of you. Biru just left his mom and littermates; some crying is grief, not manipulation. Put the crate right next to your bed so he can hear you breathe. A ticking clock or a warm (not hot) wrapped heat pack mimics littermates.

Do a final potty trip right before lights out. If he cries at 2am, he probably genuinely needs to go — carry him out, no talking, no play, potty, straight back in the crate. Boring is the message: night is for sleeping.

It gets better fast. Most puppies settle within a week when the routine never wavers.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a biewer puppy this tiny can't make it through the night at 10 weeks — expect one 2–3am potty run for the first couple of weeks. It's temporary. Truly.
