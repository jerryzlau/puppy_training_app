Here's the truth about biewers: their bladder is the size of a walnut. Potty training a toy breed is famously slow — not because they're dumb (they're not), but because they physically cannot hold it long. The fix is a schedule so strict it feels silly.

Take him out: right after waking, right after eating or drinking, right after play, and every 1–2 hours in between. Go to the same spot, stand there boring as a lamppost, and the instant he finishes — party. Treat within two seconds, praise, the works.

Misses are YOUR misses (too long between trips). Never punish an accident; just clean with enzyme cleaner so the smell doesn't invite a repeat.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** in rain or cold a biewer may simply refuse to go outside. Have a backup indoor potty pad in a fixed spot from day one — many biewer owners keep both systems for life.
