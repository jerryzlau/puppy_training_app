Assemble the portfolio into a show. Pick five tricks that flow: spin to warm up, leg weaves as the centerpiece, high five for audience participation, "bang!" for drama, wave goodbye as the closer. Chain them with tiny pauses — each cue lands after the applause, not during.

Rehearse with both of you as co-handlers; a trick hand-off (he weaves your legs, then delivers a toy to Mei, then dies theatrically at her feet) is production value no golden retriever act can match, because none of them are this portable.

Then: audience. Grandparents on a video call count. Film it properly — this is the graduation performance, and future-you will rewatch it an embarrassing number of times.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers are hams who feed on audience energy — but they also read audience *boredom*. Keep the show under 90 seconds and leave the crowd wanting more. Showbiz rules.
