Impulse control graduates from party games to daily life this week. Two rituals, practiced at every natural opportunity:

**The bowl.** Ask for a sit. Lower the bowl. If his butt lifts, the bowl silently rises again — no scolding, the bowl simply obeys gravity in reverse. When he holds the sit until the bowl lands: "free!", dinner is served. Three days of consistency and he'll sit for dinner like it's his idea.

**The door.** Doors are where tiny dogs get stepped on and where dashers are born. At every interior door: "wait," door opens a crack, closes if he surges, opens again. You step through first, then "free!". This isn't dominance theater — it's a safety habit that one day stops him at an open front door with a street beyond it.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** underfoot is the most dangerous place a biewer lives. A dog who defaults to *sit and wait* at doors and kitchen counters is a dog who doesn't get accidentally punted.
