First leash lessons happen where it's boring: your living room. Clip the lightest leash you can find to the harness and just let him drag it around (supervised — leashes snag). It's furniture now, not news.

Then pick it up and become interesting: pat your leg, shuffle backward, treat him every time he arrives at your ankle. Twenty steps of "hanging out near the human on a loose leash" is the entire foundation of every good walk.

Never drag him. If he plants, you've lost his brain — get closer, get happier, lower the bar. The leash is a safety line, not a steering wheel.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** buy the lightest leash made — a heavy clip alone weighs on a 4-pound puppy's harness like a barbell and teaches him the leash is a burden.
