Here's the deal you're offering Biru: *a loose leash makes the world move; a tight leash makes the world stop.* Enforce it with the patience of a saint and it becomes physics to him.

The moment the leash tightens: stop walking. Just stand there, boring. The instant it softens — even because he turned to see why you stopped — "yes!", walk on. That's it. That's the whole technique. It feels slow for three days and then suddenly you have a dog who self-corrects.

Meanwhile, make your left ankle the best real estate on the street: random treats delivered right at the seam of your shoe. Dogs hang out where the vending machine is.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a biewer pulling on a harness looks harmless — resist the urge to just let it slide. A 4-pound puller becomes a leash-tangling, ankle-orbiting chaos machine. The rules are the rules, even for cute.
