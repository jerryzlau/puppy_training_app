Week one built the crate; this week finishes it into what it should be for life: Biru's own room, chosen voluntarily.

Add the cue: "bedtime" (or whatever you like), toss nothing, and wait — he knows this game. Pay when he enters, pay extra when he settles. Practice closed-door time *while you're home and visible*: dinner on the couch, him in the crate with a chew, ten calm minutes. Crating must not only predict departures, or it becomes a departure alarm.

The graduation signal costs you nothing to spot: one day you'll look over and find he's put himself to bed in the open crate. When that happens, the crate has stopped being furniture and become his address.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers nest — add a soft blanket he can burrow into and you'll double the crate's pull. Wash it rarely (his smell is the point) and never as punishment cleanup.
