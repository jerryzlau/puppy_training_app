Here is the toy-dog trap: Biru is so cute that strangers will *reward his worst behavior*. Jumping, spinning, demanding — "aww, it's fine, he's tiny!" It is not fine; it's how adorable puppies become obnoxious adults.

The rule is four on the floor: paws down (or better, a sit) is the only position that earns petting. When admirers approach — and with a biewer, they will — you become the bouncer: "He's in training! Give him a second to sit first." Most people love helping. If someone swoops anyway and he jumps, calmly step back with him and reset.

At home, same rules apply to guests: Biru on leash for arrivals, sit earns hellos, jumping makes visitors turn into boring statues (brief your friends — they're training equipment now).

⚠️ **biewer tip:** looming over a tiny dog is scary — teach greeters to crouch sideways and offer a hand low, rather than reaching down over his head like a claw machine.
