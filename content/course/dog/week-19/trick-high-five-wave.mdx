Two tricks for the price of one, built from week 12's "shake." High five first: take the shake, rotate your palm to face him, raise it a few inches, cue "high five!" — same motion, cooler branding. Pay contact with the palm.

Wave is high five with the target removed: offer the palm slightly farther away and mark the paw-lift *before* contact. He flaps at air; you cheer like it's Wimbledon. Separate cue ("bye-bye!" with your own wave) or he'll merge them into one confused paw-flail.

Chain the set: sit, high five, wave goodbye. That's a complete talk-show exit, and yes, this is what all the training was for.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers naturally paw when they want things — capture those offered paw-lifts with a mark-and-pay and this week trains itself.
