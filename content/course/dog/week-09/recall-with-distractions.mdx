Time to run the gauntlet. On the long line, deliberately practice recalls *away from* increasingly interesting things: first a fascinating bush, then a passing stranger, then a distant dog. Each success gets the triple-jackpot and — this is sneaky and important — *often gets released right back to the fun*: "come!" → party → "go sniff!". Recall that ends fun kills recall; recall that's a toll booth on the way back to fun gets paid happily forever.

Rank his distraction hierarchy honestly. For most biewers: smells < strangers < dogs < that one specific enemy pigeon. Work up the ladder; skipping rungs teaches failure.

By the end of the week you want one unplanned, real-world recall under your belt — the moment you realize the button actually works.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** other dogs are usually a biewer's #1 distraction — they're social little gremlins. Buy extra distance from dog-distractions at first; success at 40 feet builds the recall that later works at 10.
