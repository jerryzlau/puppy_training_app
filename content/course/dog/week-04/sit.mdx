The classic, and Biru is ready. Hold a treat to his nose, lift it slowly up and back over his head — as the nose goes up, the butt goes down. The moment it touches: "yes!", treat, party.

After a dozen smooth lure reps, get the treat out of your hand: same hand motion, empty hand, treat from the other hand. Then add the word — say "sit," pause a beat, then the hand signal. Soon the word alone works.

Start cashing it in immediately: sit for the food bowl, sit for the leash, sit for the door. "Sit" becomes his way of saying *please* — a dog who knows how to ask politely rarely needs to demand rudely.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** don't push his rear down, ever — a biewer puppy's joints are delicate, and hands-on molding teaches nothing anyway. Let the lure do the work.
