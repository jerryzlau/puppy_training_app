The final boss of city-dog skills: doing absolutely nothing, in public, on purpose. A dog who can settle under a café table opens up your whole life — brunches, patios, travel.

Build it at home on a distinct travel mat: "go to your mat" → lure onto it → down → slow trickle of treats *for staying put*. The mat becomes a treat-generating magic carpet. Extend the calm: treats every few seconds, then every 30, then only occasionally, while you sit beside him scrolling your phone (authentic training conditions).

Then take the show on the road, ramping difficulty: quiet bench → slow coffee shop patio at off-hours → real brunch. Bring the mat, a chew, and low expectations. Fifteen calm minutes is a triumph; leave before he unravels.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** under-the-table beats on-your-lap for learning settle — lap dogs stay on alert scanning the room from the best vantage point. Down on the mat, world muffled by tablecloth, biewers actually nap.
