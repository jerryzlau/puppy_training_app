City life is a sensory assault at ankle height: buses hiss, grates clang, elevators do whatever elevators do. This week you methodically make each one boring, using the same recipe as week 4 — distance + treats + retreat while it's going well.

Elevators: ride with him in your arms first (height = confidence), treats flowing, then a ride with paws on the floor. Loud streets: find the distance where he notices trucks but still takes food, feed through each rumble, and creep closer over days — never all at once.

Keep a running list of his "haunted objects" (every dog has some — a flapping awning, a specific statue) and give each one the treatment: approach at his pace, pay generously, never force contact.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** sidewalk grates are genuinely dangerous for biewer-sized paws, not just scary — teach him to walk around them (guide with the leash, pay the detour) rather than over them, as a lifelong habit.
