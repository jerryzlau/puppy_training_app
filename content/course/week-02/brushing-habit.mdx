That silky biewer coat is hair, not fur — it grows long, it tangles, and it mats close to the skin where you can't see. The only defense is a short daily brushing habit, and the only way that habit is pleasant is if you build it now, while he's a baby.

Show the brush → treat. One stroke → treat. Two strokes → treat. Keep sessions to two minutes and end while he's still happy — a hundred short good sessions beat one long bad one.

Use a pin brush for the body and learn the "line brushing" idea: part the hair, brush from the skin out, section by section. Right now the coat is short and easy; the habit is what you're grooming.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** always mist the coat lightly with water or conditioner spray before brushing — dry-brushing a biewer coat breaks the hair and actually creates tangles.
