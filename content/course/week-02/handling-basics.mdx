Biewers need lifelong grooming — brushing, nail trims, teeth cleaning, face wiping. A dog who panics at being handled makes every one of those a wrestling match for the next 15 years. So we start now, absurdly gently.

Touch a paw. Treat. Touch an ear. Treat. Lift a lip. Treat. One second each, always followed by food, always stopping while he's still relaxed. You're not grooming anything yet — you're teaching "hands on my body predict cheese."

Watch his body: loose and wiggly means continue, stiff or head-turning means you went too fast. Shrink the touch, raise the pay.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** the face is the sensitive zone — biewers need daily eye-area wiping to prevent tear staining, so put extra reps into gentle face touches this week.
