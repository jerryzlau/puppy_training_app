Take the heel outside, where it earns its keep. The structure that works: most of the walk stays free-range sniffing (his walk, his newspaper), and short heel bursts handle the moments that need control — doorways, crowded stretches, passing that one bakery.

Cue "heel," get five beautiful steps past the temptation, then release with "go sniff!" — the release is the payment. Heel that always ends in freedom stays enthusiastic; heel that means "fun is over" goes limp.

Stack difficulty slowly: quiet block, then busier, then past dogs. Every clean heel-past is a rep in the bank for the day you truly need it.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** five heel-steps for a biewer is a sprint of concentration — his legs take four strides to your one. Keep bursts short and celebrate like he cleared a jump course.
