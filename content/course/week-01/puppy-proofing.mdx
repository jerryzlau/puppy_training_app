Before the cuddles: get on your hands and knees and see the house the way a four-pound puppy does. Loose cords look like chew toys. A dropped ibuprofen is an emergency. A gap behind the couch is a den.

Set up one "safe zone" — an exercise pen or gated corner with his bed, a water bowl, and two chew toys. This is where Biru lives when nobody can actively watch him. It's not a punishment; it's how you prevent bad habits from ever being rehearsed.

Rotate his toys so novelty stays high, and keep one "jackpot" chew for the pen only, so the pen predicts good things.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** biewers are small enough to squeeze into gaps you'd never suspect — behind appliances, under recliners. Block anything wider than your fist, and treat recliner chairs as off-limits forever.
