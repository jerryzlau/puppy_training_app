The crate is Biru's bedroom, never his jail. Start with the door open and make it rain: toss a treat in, let him walk in, eat, walk out. Repeat until he's diving in happily.

Next, feed his meals in there. Then start closing the door for just a few seconds while he's eating, opening it before he cares. Build up slowly — seconds, then a minute, then a few.

Never open the door while he's crying (that teaches "crying opens doors"). Wait for two seconds of quiet, then open calmly. And never use the crate as punishment — it must always predict good things.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** toy breeds get cold easily. Line the crate with a fleece blanket and keep it out of drafts — a cozy crate is half the battle for a biewer.
