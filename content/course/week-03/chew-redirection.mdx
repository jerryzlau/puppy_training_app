Teething is coming, and Biru's gums are going to ache. He *will* chew — the only question is what. Your job is to make the right answer the easy answer.

Keep three approved chews with different textures (rubber, soft rope, a puppy-safe teething chew) in every room he uses. When he gnaws a chair leg or a shoe: no drama. Trade him — offer the toy, praise madly when he takes it. Chasing him for the stolen sock is the best game he's ever invented; a boring trade beats it every time.

Frozen wet washcloth twists are teething gold. So are frozen carrots (tiny ones, supervised).

⚠️ **biewer tip:** size chews for a tiny jaw — anything harder than a fingernail-press can crack toy-breed teeth. Skip antlers and hard nylon entirely.
