Puppy teeth are needles and Biru will use them — on hands, ankles, and the drawstrings of your favorite hoodie. This is normal and important: right now he's learning how much jaw pressure is too much, and the only teacher available is you.

When teeth hit skin too hard: a calm "ouch," then all fun stops for three seconds. No yelling, no pushing (pushing is a game). Then offer a toy — "that, not me." If he keeps coming back hard, calmly end the play session entirely.

The goal isn't zero mouthing yet; it's a progressively softer mouth. Full "no teeth on humans" comes as adult teeth arrive.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** never play rough with hands with a toy breed — what's cute at 4 pounds becomes a bite-risk habit in a dog whose warning nips can still break skin. Toys are for teeth; hands are for petting.
