Your normal recall will be great. But build one more layer: an *emergency* word, reserved for the once-a-year moment a door blows open onto a busy street. It works because it's never diluted — no casual use, no unpaid reps, no "eh, good enough" rewards.

Pick something you'd never say otherwise — "PARTY!", "JACKPOT!", the sillier the better. Charging it is pure Pavlov: say the word, then immediately deliver the best food that exists in your house (real chicken, cheese, the forbidden stuff). Ten reps over a few days. He doesn't have to *do* anything yet; the word just predicts miracles.

Then test cold, once: from another room, out of nowhere. Thunderpaws incoming = it's loaded. Re-charge with a few reps monthly, forever, and guard it like the fire extinguisher it is.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a biewer bolting is genuinely hard to grab — they're low, fast, and dodge like rabbits. The emergency word that makes him *run at you* beats every lunge-and-grab you'll ever attempt.
