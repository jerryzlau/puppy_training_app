Recall moves outside this week, and outside is where recalls go to die: squirrels, smells, other dogs, wind. The bridge between living-room hero and off-leash dog is the long line — 15 feet of light lead that lets him range while physics guarantees he can't fail beyond arm's reach.

Quiet park, low distractions. Let him drift and sniff, then hit him with your best "Biru, come!" — and here's the discipline: *only call when you'd bet money on it.* If he's mid-sniff on something fascinating, wait. Every ignored recall teaches him the word is optional; every successful one, jackpot-paid, hardens it into reflex.

If he doesn't come, don't repeat the cue — gently reel the line while sweet-talking, then pay anyway, then lower the difficulty.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** clip the long line to the *harness*, never a collar — a full-tilt biewer hitting the end of 15 feet on his neck is exactly the trachea injury we've been avoiding all course.
