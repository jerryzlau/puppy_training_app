Rule one of walking a biewer: the leash never, ever attaches to a neck collar. A toy breed's trachea is soft and tiny; one hard lunge against a collar can cause real damage. Collars are for ID tags. Walks happen on a harness.

Choose a Y-front step-in or vest harness sized for toy breeds. Fitting: snug enough that two fingers slide under, loose enough that nothing rubs behind his front legs.

Then sell it to him: harness appears → treats appear. Head through the loop → treat. Clip → treat → immediately off. Build up to him wearing it around the house while great things happen. A dog who dives *into* his harness makes every walk for the next 15 years easier.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** that silky coat mats under harness straps — brush the armpit and chest areas after every wear, and take the harness off at home.
