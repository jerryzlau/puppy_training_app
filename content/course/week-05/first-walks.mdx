The first outdoor walks are not exercise — they're field trips. The goal is a puppy who thinks *outside* is wonderful, not a puppy who covers distance.

Keep it short (ten minutes is plenty at this age) and let him sniff. Sniffing is how dogs read the newspaper; a walk that's all march and no sniff is like being dragged through a library too fast to read.

Bring the best treats and pay generously for check-ins — every time he glances at you unprompted, "yes!" and treat. You're planting the seed that walks are a *together* activity. If he freezes or won't take food, he's overwhelmed: pick him up, go home, try a quieter street tomorrow.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** watch the ground temperature and the weather — a biewer gets cold fast and hot pavement burns tiny paws in seconds. In winter, a sweater isn't fashion; it's function.
