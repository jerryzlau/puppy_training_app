Level up. Place a treat on the floor and cover it with your flat hand. Say "leave it" once. When he stops trying to excavate your palm and pulls back — "yes!", pay from the other hand. Progress to hovering your hand an inch above the treat, then to a fully uncovered treat with your hand ready to cover it if he dives.

Then make it real: "accidentally" drop a piece of kibble while making dinner. "Leave it." The floor food scenario is the one that actually matters — that's where the dropped grape, the medication, the mystery chicken bone will appear one day.

Move the game to different rooms; dogs don't generalize automatically, and a leave-it that only works in the kitchen is a kitchen decoration.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** keep sessions under 5 minutes with rice-grain treats — impulse control is genuinely *tiring* for a puppy brain, and a fried biewer gets zoomies, not wisdom.
