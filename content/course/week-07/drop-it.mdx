"Drop it" is "leave it"'s sibling: leave-it is *don't pick it up*, drop-it is *spit out what's already in the mouth*. With a curious ground-level vacuum like a biewer, you need both.

Start as a trade, not a theft. He has a toy; you present a treat at his nose; the toy falls out (physics — can't chew and sniff simultaneously); "drop it," treat, and here's the crucial part: *give the toy back* most of the time. Drop-it that usually means "brief pause, then double prize" builds a dog who spits things out happily. Drop-it that means "you lose your stuff" builds a dog who swallows evidence and runs.

Never chase, never pry his mouth open unless it's a true emergency — both teach him to gulp faster next time.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** a biewer's mouth is small but determined — chicken bones on the sidewalk are the classic city hazard. Drill drop-it *before* week 10's café outings, not after the first incident.
