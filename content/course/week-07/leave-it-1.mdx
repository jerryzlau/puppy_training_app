Close a boring treat in your fist and offer it. Biru will lick, paw, and nibble at your knuckles — say nothing, do nothing, be a statue. The instant he gives up and pulls back, even a centimeter: "yes!" and pay him a *better* treat from your other hand.

He is learning the most counterintuitive lesson in dog school: *backing off the thing gets you the thing (or better).* Never feed him the treat he was mugging — the prize always comes from elsewhere, so the "leave it" treat stays theoretical.

Once he backs off fast, wait for the bonus: that little glance up at your face. Pay that glance like a jackpot. "When in doubt, check in with my human" is the entire philosophy of a well-trained dog, in one look.

⚠️ **biewer tip:** use rice-grain-sized treats and keep sessions under 5 minutes — a biewer's daily calorie budget is about that of a hamster with ambitions.
