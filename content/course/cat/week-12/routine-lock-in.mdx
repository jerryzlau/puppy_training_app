Skills decay politely and quietly; schedules don't. This week you write the constitution: daily hunt sessions, the weekly spa, monthly weigh-in, carrier permanently on display, training minutes tucked before dinner.

Everything goes in the Routine tab where both humans can see whose turn it is. The pattern strip will keep you honest about whether "we play with him constantly" is data or vibes.

Ten scheduled minutes a day is the entire maintenance budget of everything you built.

⚠️ **cat tip:** the carrier blanket gets refreshed but never washed on vet-day itself — walking into a familiar-smelling pod is half the calm.
