Graduation week opens with an audit — every skill from twelve weeks, run back to back like a tiny agility trial. Not for the certificate: rust shows you what to book for maintenance.

Anything shaky gets a note in the diary and a slot in the weekly rotation. Two minutes of upkeep preserves what took weeks to build.

Mostly, though: look at the audit list and remember week one, when this animal lived under the bed. That distance is the actual diploma.

⚠️ **cat tip:** film the audit. Besides the bragging rights, the video *is* your baseline — future-you comparing gait and jump height will thank you.
