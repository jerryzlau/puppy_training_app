If your cat is under a year old, adolescence is coming: bigger parkour, boundary testing, occasionally a swagger phase. It's normal, it's temporary, and it's much smaller with the neuter/spay handled and the hunt schedule funded.

Pick your two hills — most households choose "no counters during cooking" and "humans are never prey" — and defend those consistently while shrugging at the small stuff. Cats respect consistency and exploit committee indecision.

The extra energy is free training fuel: adolescent cats learn tricks faster than anyone.

⚠️ **cat tip:** a sudden new "bad behavior" in an adult cat is a health question before it's a discipline question — pain is the great personality-changer in cats. Vet first, then training.
