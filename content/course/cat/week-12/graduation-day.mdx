Cap and gown optional (photogenic, though). Today is a victory lap: the trick set for an audience, the fanciest wand session ending in a legendary catch, dinner served like a banquet.

Then write it down. The graduation entry with its photo shoot becomes the bookmark between "the twelve weeks we built this" and everything after.

Twelve weeks ago this was a stranger hiding in one room. Today it's a cat who comes when called, tolerates a claw trim, owns a harness, and slow-blinks at you across the couch. That's not a trained cat. That's a family member with excellent manners.

⚠️ **cat tip:** keep the diary going. The cats who stay this good at fifteen are the ones whose humans never stopped noticing — and you have a book for exactly that.
