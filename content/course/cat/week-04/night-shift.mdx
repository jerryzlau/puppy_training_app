Cats are crepuscular — wired for dawn and dusk — and a cat with unspent hunting energy spends it at 3am on your bladder. The fix is the pre-bed hunt: a real wand session ending in a catch, followed by the biggest meal of the day.

Hunt, catch, eat, groom, sleep. Run the full sequence and their biology does the rest.

The other half is harder: 3am attention must earn *nothing*. No feeding, no shouting, no chase — all of those are payment. One 3am snack buys you a month of 3am wake-ups.

⚠️ **cat tip:** an automatic feeder set for 5am beats you being the breakfast dispenser — the cat yells at the machine instead of your face, and machines don't negotiate.
