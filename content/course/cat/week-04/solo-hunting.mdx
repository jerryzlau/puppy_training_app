You can't be the entertainment committee 24/7, and a bored cat invents jobs. Solo hunting fills the gap: puzzle feeders, treat balls, boxes with treats buried in crumpled paper.

Rotate toys like a museum: three on display, the rest in storage. Last month's boring mouse comes back as a new acquisition.

The win is a cat who entertains themselves for stretches — that's not aloofness, it's a well-stocked territory doing its job.

⚠️ **cat tip:** start puzzle feeders embarrassingly easy — treats basically falling out. A cat who fails a puzzle twice files it under "not food" and never touches it again.
