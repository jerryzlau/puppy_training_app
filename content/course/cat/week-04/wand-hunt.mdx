Play for a cat isn't entertainment — it's the hunt, and a cat who doesn't hunt finds substitutes you won't like (your ankles at 3am, mostly).

Wand toys are the gold standard because prey moves *away* and hides. Drag it behind furniture, freeze it, twitch it. Erratic and evasive beats fast and constant — real mice don't do laps.

Always end with a catch and a payoff. Ending mid-chase leaves the hunt unfinished and the cat wired; catch-then-eat completes the cycle and lands them in a nap.

⚠️ **cat tip:** put wand toys away after each session. A wand lying dead on the floor all day teaches the cat it's not really prey — scarcity keeps the magic.
