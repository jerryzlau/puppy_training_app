If ankles and hands are ever prey, they are always prey. The house rule starts now: humans are furniture, toys are prey — no exceptions, even when the kitten pounce is adorable.

Catch the wind-up (the butt wiggle, the dilated eyes, the low stalk) and launch a toy across their path before the pounce lands. You're not punishing the hunt, you're aiming it.

Kicker toys — the long ones they can bunny-kick — absorb the rough play that would otherwise be your forearm.

⚠️ **cat tip:** if teeth do land on skin, freeze rather than yank. Pulling away triggers the prey reflex and finishes the bite; going boring ends the game instantly.
