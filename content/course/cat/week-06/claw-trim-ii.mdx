Scale up only as fast as the cat stays bored. A full front paw might take two sittings — fine. The moment restraint enters the picture, you're building next month's problem.

Back claws shed less and need less trimming; attempt them last and skip them if the cat votes no with their feet.

Every trim that ends calmly makes the next one easier for the rest of the cat's life. That's the actual deliverable of this week: not short claws, but a cat who thinks claw trims are dull.

⚠️ **cat tip:** if trims stay a fight, ask the vet tech for a one-visit demo — a burrito-wrap towel hold used *sparingly* is kinder than twenty failed pin-downs.
