With good real estate on offer, make the sofa a worse deal — without ever making *you* the bad guy. Double-sided tape or a draped blanket on the favorite corner does the arguing for you.

Catch them mid-sofa? No shout. Walk over, lure them to the post with a treat or a dangled toy, pay when claws hit sisal. The lesson is "this surface pays better," not "scratching is dangerous when the human watches."

Punishment teaches cats exactly one thing: do it when they're out. Redirection teaches the habit you actually want.

⚠️ **cat tip:** scratch marks are scent posts, so clean old furniture marks with an enzyme cleaner — as long as the sofa smells like a signpost, it will be used as one.
