The week-2 paw touches were the down payment; this is the purchase. Goal for the whole week: single claws, trimmed one at a time, each one paid like it's a big deal.

Press the pad gently to pop the claw out. Trim only the clear hook at the tip — the pink core is alive and must never be nicked. One claw, jackpot, done. Ten separate mini-sessions beat one wrestling match by a mile.

A cat who's had one claw trimmed and a great snack ten times barely notices when you eventually do a whole paw.

⚠️ **cat tip:** aim for sleepy o'clock — mid-afternoon sun-puddle cat is a far more agreeable client than 6pm zoomies cat.
