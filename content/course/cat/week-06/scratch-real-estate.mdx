Scratching is non-negotiable — it's claw maintenance, stretching, and territorial signage in one. You can't remove the behavior; you can only decide where it happens.

The post must beat the sofa on its own merits: taller than a full stretch, absolutely stable, wrapped in sisal. Put it *next to* the crime scene, not in a far corner — the location is part of what's being marked.

Pay every observed use. You're bidding for real estate against your own furniture; make the post the better deal.

⚠️ **cat tip:** cats fall into vertical scratchers and horizontal scratchers, and many are both. Offer one of each before concluding your cat "won't use a post".
