Every strange thing a vet does can be pre-paid at home. Lip lifts, ear peeks, paw squeezes, the two-handed torso feel — thirty seconds of rehearsal a day makes the real exam a rerun instead of an ambush.

Do a table version too: cats find elevated strange surfaces alarming, and the exam table is both. A yoga mat on the counter, treats on arrival, exam-lite, done.

You're not making the vet visit fun. You're making it *familiar*, which for a cat is nearly the same thing.

⚠️ **cat tip:** rehearse the waiting room as well — carrier sits on a chair beside you, treats through the door, five minutes of nothing happening. The lobby is half the stress.
