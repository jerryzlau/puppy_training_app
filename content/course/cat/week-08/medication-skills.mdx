Someday this cat will need medicine, and that day goes smoothly only if the delivery systems were boring before they were necessary. Bank the deposits now, while nobody's sick.

Pill pockets are treats this week — no pill inside. An oral syringe of water touches the cheek, pays out. If a blank pocket only ever means snack, the future pill has camouflage.

Practice the towel burrito once: a snug wrap, three seconds, release, jackpot. Rehearsed calm beats improvised restraint at 2am.

⚠️ **cat tip:** never medicate in the cat's food bowl — one bitter meal can put a cat off that food entirely, and a cat off their food is its own emergency.
