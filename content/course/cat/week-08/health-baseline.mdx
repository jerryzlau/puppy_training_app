You can't spot "off" without knowing "normal." This week you write the baseline: weight, resting breathing rate (under 30/min asleep), how much water, how the litter box traffic runs.

Weight is the big one — a half-kilo drift on a small cat is enormous and invisible to daily eyes. Monthly weigh-ins catch what cuddles can't.

It all goes in the Routine tab. Vets make dramatically better calls when "he's drinking more than usual" comes with numbers and dates.

⚠️ **cat tip:** more thirst, more pee, and weight loss in a middle-aged cat is the classic kidney/thyroid signature — caught early on a log like this one, it's very manageable.
