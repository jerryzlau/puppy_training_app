For most cats the sequence carrier → car → vet is one long horror movie. We're re-cutting the film: most car trips now end at home with snacks, and nothing else happens.

Parked car, engine off, carrier seat-belted in the footwell or on the seat. Two minutes, home, jackpot. Then engine on. Then around the block.

Cover the carrier with a light towel — less visual chaos lowers the volume of everything.

⚠️ **cat tip:** seat-belt the carrier or wedge it in the footwell; a carrier that slides at the first corner undoes three sessions of goodwill. Stable floor, stable cat.
