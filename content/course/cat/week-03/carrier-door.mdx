Only start this lesson once the carrier is genuinely boring. Close the door while they're mid-treat, count five, open it before they finish. The door becoming irrelevant is the point.

Build in small jumps: thirty seconds, a minute, a carry around the house. Set the carrier down gently, open it, and let them exit at their own pace — no dumping.

If any step causes scrambling or crying, you skipped a step. Drop back to the last easy version and park there for a few days.

⚠️ **cat tip:** carry the carrier with both arms hugged to your chest like a fragile parcel, not swinging by the handle — the swaying is half of what cats hate about travel.
