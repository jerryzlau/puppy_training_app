Some cats love being held; many tolerate it; a few file lawsuits. Wherever yours lands, a calm lift matters for emergencies, so we build it in tiny pieces.

Hands on sides, count two, release, pay. Then a one-inch lift. Then a real hold: one hand under the chest, one under the back legs — dangling legs are why cats hate amateur holds.

Always put them down *before* the wiggle. The cat learns that being held always ends promptly and on their terms, which paradoxically makes them tolerate it longer.

⚠️ **cat tip:** hold the cat facing outward, hindquarters supported like a shelf. Facing your chest feels like restraint; facing out with a stable base feels like a perch.
