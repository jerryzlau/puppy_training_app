For most cats the carrier means one thing: the vet. This week we delete that association. The carrier lives in the living room now, door off or tied open, cozy blanket inside, treats appearing in it as if by magic.

Never close the door this week. Never chase the cat into it. The entire job is to make the carrier furniture — boring, safe, occasionally profitable.

A cat who chooses to nap in the carrier has rewritten the story. That's the milestone, and it can't be rushed.

⚠️ **cat tip:** pick a carrier that opens from the top as well as the front, and whose top half unclips. At the vet, a top-opening carrier means the exam can start with the cat still sitting in their own familiar bottom half.
