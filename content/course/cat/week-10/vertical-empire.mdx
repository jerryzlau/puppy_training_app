Floor space means little to a cat; a home is measured in *levels*. Every high perch you add is square footage annexed to the empire, and height is confidence — the surveying cat is a relaxed cat.

Cheap wins: a cleared bookcase top with a towel, a window hammock, one wall shelf near existing furniture. The route up matters as much as the perch.

A window with bird traffic is cat television, and it runs all day for free.

⚠️ **cat tip:** in a multi-pet home, high ground is the cat's safe country — make at least one perch unreachable by the dog, the toddler, and the vacuum.
