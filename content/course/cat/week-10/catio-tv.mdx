Round out the empire across every sense. Sight: a feeder outside the window turns glass into live television. Smell: catnip works on only two-thirds of cats — silvervine and valerian catch most of the rest. Touch: tunnels, crinkle mats, sisal, fleece.

Offer novelty gently and one thing at a time, and watch what your specific cat actually loves. The diary is for exactly this — log the hits.

An enriched territory is the vaccine against almost every behavior problem in the book: boredom is the root of most of them.

⚠️ **cat tip:** rub silvervine sticks between your palms to wake the scent up before offering — a dusty stick from the packet is often a dud.
