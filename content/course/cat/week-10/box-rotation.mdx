The box obsession is real estate plus ambush cover plus insulation, all free. Lean into it as an enrichment program: new box, few days of glory, box vanishes, different box appears. Novelty on rotation.

Paper bags (handles cut off — heads get stuck), crumpled packing paper, and a box with treat-holes cut in the sides are a full amusement park for the price of your recycling.

Novelty is the active ingredient. The same box forever becomes furniture; the *rotation* is what keeps the territory interesting.

⚠️ **cat tip:** a box arriving in a new room also makes strange rooms feel safer — deploy one as a mobile embassy when you want the cat to expand territory.
