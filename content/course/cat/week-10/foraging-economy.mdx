A wild cat makes a dozen small hunts a day; a house cat gets a full bowl and twenty-two empty hours. Moving food from bowl to *work* fills the day with jobs and slows the gobble-and-yak eaters.

Rotate the difficulty: treat balls, muffin-tin-and-toy puzzles, treats hidden along the climbing routes, kibble scattered for a floor hunt.

The bowl doesn't have to die — but the cat with a foraging economy has plans, and a cat with plans doesn't invent them on your curtains.

⚠️ **cat tip:** hide a few treats at perch level too — vertical foraging chains the empire and the economy together.
