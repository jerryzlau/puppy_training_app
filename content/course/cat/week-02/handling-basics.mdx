The goal this week is simple: every human touch predicts something good, and every touch stops *before* the cat wants it to. Short sessions, always ending with the cat wanting more.

Start where cats like it: cheeks, chin, base of the ears. The back is next. The belly is a trap in most cats — fur down there is for trusted friends only, and often not even them.

Watch the tail. Soft and still is happy; twitching tip is "wrapping up now"; thumping is "you missed the memo." Stop at the first twitch and you'll be allowed longer next time.

⚠️ **cat tip:** paw touches feel weird to cats but matter enormously — claw trims depend on them. One second, one treat, done. You're buying stock in week 6.
