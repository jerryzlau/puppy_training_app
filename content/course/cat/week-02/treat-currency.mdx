Training a cat runs on the same engine as training a dog — payment — but the currency is fussier. Test broadly: freeze-dried chicken, lickable tubes, a morsel of plain cooked fish. Watch for the wide-eyed grab versus the polite sniff.

Once you know the top tier, guard it. High-value treats only appear for training and hard things like the carrier. If they're free all day they stop being money.

Palm-feeding builds trust; following the hand is the seed of every cue you'll teach later.

⚠️ **cat tip:** cat treats should stay under 10% of daily calories, and a cat's daily budget is tiny. Break treats into pea-sized pieces — enthusiasm tracks flavor, not size.
