Most litter box problems are setup problems. The formula: one box per cat plus one spare, big enough for a full turn-around, in a quiet spot where nobody can be ambushed mid-use.

Most cats prefer unscented, clumping litter a couple of inches deep. Skip the liners and the perfumes — they're for humans.

Scoop daily. A dirty box is the number-one reason cats take their business elsewhere, and every out-of-box accident makes the next one more likely.

⚠️ **cat tip:** covered boxes trap smell inside and block sight lines out — many cats hate them for the same reason you'd hate a porta-potty with no lock. If in doubt, take the lid off.
