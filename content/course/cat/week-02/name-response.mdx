Cats learn their names — they're just honest about cost-benefit. Make the name worth answering: say it once, and the instant they look, mark with a cheerful "yes!" and pay.

Never use the name for anything bad. Not the carrier, not medicine, not the vacuum apology tour. The name predicts good things or it predicts nothing.

Level up distance slowly: one meter, across the room, out of sight. A cat who comes when called is not a myth — it's a cat with a reliable payment history.

⚠️ **cat tip:** cats respond best to higher-pitched, up-swinging voices. The "pspsps" works in every language for a reason — use the same bright tone for the name.
