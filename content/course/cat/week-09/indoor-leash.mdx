A cat walk is not a dog walk — you follow the cat. Indoors first: harness on, leash dragging, then leash in hand while the cat leads and you provide slack.

Never pull. Leash pressure on a cat triggers park-brake mode or panic, neither useful. The leash is a safety line, not a steering wheel; your steering tools are treats and the target stick from week 5.

Sessions end while the cat's still into it, snack bar closes with the harness.

⚠️ **cat tip:** practice picking up the leashed cat smoothly (week 3 hold). Outside, "lift and relocate" is your answer to every dog, car, and surprise — it needs to be reflex-fast and drama-free.
