Repetition turns terror into territory. Same door, same first few meters, same time-ish of day — within a handful of trips the cat has annexed the route and patrols it like a landlord.

Build a strict exit ritual: harness goes on at one spot, one treat at the threshold, out. The ritual matters in reverse too — a cat who only exits harnessed doesn't door-dash naked.

Log outings in Routine. The diary's pattern strip will show you the hour your cat actually likes for adventures.

⚠️ **cat tip:** never let a harnessed cat lead you out of sight of home base — confidence outruns geography fast, and a spooked cat bolts along the route they know, not toward you.
