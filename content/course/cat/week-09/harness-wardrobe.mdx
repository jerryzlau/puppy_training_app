Cat harness training starts embarrassingly small, because a cat's first reaction to wearing a thing is the flop of betrayal. Fabric on floor → sniff → payday. Draped, not clipped. Clipped for seconds, not minutes.

Use an escape-resistant style (H-harness or vest, snug enough to fit two fingers under, no more). Cats are liquid; a loose harness is a suggestion.

Treats flow *only* while the harness is on. Off means the snack bar closes — this single rule does most of the work.

⚠️ **cat tip:** the dramatic slow-motion fall-over when first wearing a harness is normal proprioception weirdness, not suffering. Lure a step with a treat; walking usually reboots the cat.
