"Go to your mat" is the politest trick in the book: it gives the counter-surfer and the ankle-weaver a *paid position* during cooking and meals.

Free-shape it: mat goes down, any interest gets clicked, standing on it pays best. Cats offer behaviors fast when a mat is clearly the hot spot on the floor.

Build distance and duration separately. The endgame is a cat parked regally on a mat by the kitchen door — occupied, paid, out of the pan.

⚠️ **cat tip:** the mat is portable calm. The same mat at the vet, in a hotel, or at grandma's imports "this spot pays" into any strange room.
