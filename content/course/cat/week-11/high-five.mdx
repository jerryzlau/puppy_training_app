Pure showing off this week — which is to say, pure concentrated training practice while everyone's warmed up. The high five falls out of the week-2 paw work: closed fist with a treat, and most cats bat at it. Click the lift.

Shift from fist to open palm, add the words once the motion is smooth. Three in a row on cue is the graduation bar.

Two-minute sessions, always ending on a win. Trick training is trust-building wearing a party hat.

⚠️ **cat tip:** left-pawed and right-pawed cats are real — if progress stalls, offer the other side and watch it click.
