Week 5 planted the recall; now we proof it against real life. Call mid-play, call from upstairs, call while a guest is mid-chin-scratch. Every success at a new difficulty gets the jackpot rate.

If a call fails, don't repeat the cue into the void — go closer, make it easier, pay the win. Every ignored cue quietly teaches ignoring; every paid cue compounds.

This is the skill you'll use the day a door blows open. Fund it accordingly.

⚠️ **cat tip:** keep one sound sacred — a specific treat-jar shake or bag crinkle that *always* ends in payment, even when the cue gets you both to the kitchen at 2am. Emergency recall runs on sacred sounds.
