Cats trust people who play hard to get. The guest protocol: visitors ignore the cat totally — no reaching, no eye contact, no "pspsps." The cat gets to do the approaching, which is why cat-indifferent guests always get chosen.

Arm guests with treats to toss casually on the floor, no strings attached. Approach happens on cat schedule, sometimes visit three.

A cat who greets guests wasn't born social — they've just never been grabbed by one.

⚠️ **cat tip:** give the cat a high perch in the social room. Most "hides all visit" cats become "supervises from the bookshelf" cats when watching from altitude is an option.
