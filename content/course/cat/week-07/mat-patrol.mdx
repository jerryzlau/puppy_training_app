Mats aren't a cosmetic issue — they pull on skin with every step and can hide sores underneath. The usual suspects: armpits, behind the ears, the belly fringe, and the fluffy trousers.

Tease small mats apart with fingers, holding the base so nothing tugs skin. A wide-tooth comb splits bigger ones. Never scissors — cat skin is paper-thin, tents up into mats, and cuts far more easily than you think.

A weekly comb-through means mats never get big enough to matter.

⚠️ **cat tip:** for a serious matted patch, the answer is clippers at a groomer or vet, not a longer fight at home. One bad scissor accident costs more trust than ten professional shaves.
