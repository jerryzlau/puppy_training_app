Dental disease hits most cats by age three, and brushing is the only real prevention — but it's a long diplomatic project. This week is treaty negotiation, not war.

Pet toothpaste only (xylitol-free, meat flavored — cats consider it a treat). Let them lick it off your finger for days before that finger ever approaches the mouth.

Gum line, three seconds, done, party. The bar for success is "the cat didn't leave," and that bar is fine.

⚠️ **cat tip:** the outsides of the upper back teeth collect the most tartar and are — conveniently — the easiest to reach. If you only ever brush one zone, make it that one.
