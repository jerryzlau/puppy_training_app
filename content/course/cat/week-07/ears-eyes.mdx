The weekly once-over is how you catch problems while they're cheap: ear gunk, eye discharge, hidden mats, mystery lumps, flea dirt. Cats hide illness professionally — your hands find what your eyes won't.

Make it a massage with an agenda. Cheeks, ears (peek inside), eyes, chin — then the full body pass, feeling for anything new.

Healthy ears are pale pink and nearly odorless. Dark crumbs or a yeasty smell is a vet call, not a home project.

⚠️ **cat tip:** log the once-over in the Routine tab. "Found this lump — was it here last month?" is a question your diary can actually answer.
