Time to chain the pieces: brush, once-over, a couple of claw tips — one relaxed sitting, treats throughout, the cat free to leave at any point (that freedom is *why* they stay).

Keep the whole thing under ten minutes. It's maintenance, not a production; frequency beats thoroughness in every category.

Book it as a weekly routine item. A standing spa day means nothing — claws, mats, teeth, lumps — ever drifts more than a week out of date.

⚠️ **cat tip:** same place, same towel, same treats each week. The towel becomes the cue, and many cats start showing up to the spa on their own.
