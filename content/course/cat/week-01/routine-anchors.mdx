Cats are creatures of pattern. The fastest way to make a new house feel predictable is to anchor the day: same feeding times, same evening play session, same wind-down.

Anticipation is the proof. When the cat starts waiting at the food bowl before you get there, their internal clock has accepted the new schedule — and a cat with a reliable schedule is a calmer cat.

This is also your first diary data: log meals and play in the Routine tab and the patterns will show themselves within a week.

⚠️ **cat tip:** feed the evening meal *after* the play session — hunt, catch, eat, groom, sleep is the natural sequence, and it buys you a quieter night.
