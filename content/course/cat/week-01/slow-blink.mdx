Cats speak a quieter language than dogs, and the fastest way to earn trust is to speak it back. The slow blink is cat for "I'm no threat": look at them softly, close your eyes slowly, open them slowly. Getting one back is a real milestone.

Offer a finger at nose height and let *them* close the distance. A sniff is hello; a cheek rub is them marking you as safe territory — a genuine compliment.

Never force contact. Every interaction the cat chooses builds the bank; every one you impose makes a withdrawal.

⚠️ **cat tip:** approach from the side and crouch down — looming straight on reads as predator. Small humans are less scary humans.
