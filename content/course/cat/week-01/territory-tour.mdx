Once the safe room is boring, open the door — but don't carry the cat out. Let them do the exploring, one room at a time, always able to retreat to base.

Follow at a distance. If something startles them and they bolt back to the safe room, that's the system working, not a setback. The retreat is what makes the bravery possible.

Over a few days, shift the essentials to their permanent spots. Keep the litter box location stable longest — moving it too early is how accidents start.

⚠️ **cat tip:** cats map territory vertically too. A cat tree or cleared windowsill in the main room gives them a safe observation post and speeds the whole process up.
