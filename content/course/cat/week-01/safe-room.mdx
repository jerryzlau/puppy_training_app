A new home is overwhelming for a cat — the kindest start is a small one. Pick one quiet room and set it up with everything: food and water (far from the litter box), the box itself, a bed, and at least one good hiding spot like a box on its side.

Let the cat explore at their own pace. Sit on the floor, read a book, exist quietly. Don't reach into hiding spots — being dragged out teaches that hiding doesn't work, which makes a more anxious cat.

When they eat a full meal with you in the room and start exploring with their tail up, they're telling you the room feels safe. Only then think about opening the door.

⚠️ **cat tip:** a cat who won't eat for more than a day is an emergency, not a phase — call the vet. Appetite is the clearest signal of how safe a cat feels.
