Recall in a cat looks like a magic trick, and it's built the same as a dog's: the cue predicts a jackpot, every time, forever. Use the charged name plus "come," pay big.

Never call for anything the cat dislikes. If it's carrier time, go collect them — spend from the trust bank, don't poison the recall.

A cat who reliably appears at a call is a cat you can find in an emergency, an open-door scare, a vet-day morning. It's worth every treat it costs.

⚠️ **cat tip:** run one "fire drill" recall a week from a different room and pay triple. Rare surprise jackpots are what make a recall survive real-world distractions.
