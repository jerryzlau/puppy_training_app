The nose-touch is the master key of cat training. Cats investigate new things nose-first, so hold out a finger just ahead of them and the sniff arrives free — click the instant of contact.

Once touching pays, move the finger: a step left, a step up onto the bench, into the carrier. You can now steer a cat anywhere without a hand ever gripping them.

Every future trick starts from this. Ten clean reps means the concept has landed.

⚠️ **cat tip:** use a chopstick as a target wand if teeth get grabby around fingers — same trick, safer hardware.
