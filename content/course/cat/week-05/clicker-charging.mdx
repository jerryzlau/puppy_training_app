The clicker (or a marker word like "yes!") is a camera shutter: it freezes the exact instant the cat earned payment. This week it means nothing yet — so we charge it like a battery.

Click, treat. Click, treat. Ten times, twice a day. Order matters: the click *causes* the treat, never together, never treat first.

You'll know it's charged when the click snaps their head toward the treat hand. Now you own a precision tool for the rest of the course.

⚠️ **cat tip:** standard clickers are loud for cat ears. Click inside your pocket to muffle it, or use a soft mouth sound — consistency matters more than volume.
