Yes, cats sit on cue — the lure is identical to the puppy version. Treat at the nose, lift it up and back over the ears; as the head follows, the back end folds. Click the instant it lands.

Add the word only after the motion is reliable: say "sit," pause, then lure. Soon the word alone works and the lure retires.

Keep sessions to two minutes. A cat's training session ends the moment they'd rather be elsewhere — always stop one rep before that.

⚠️ **cat tip:** train hungry-ish, right before mealtime. The same lesson that fights the food bowl for attention will lose; timed before dinner it's the best show in the house.
